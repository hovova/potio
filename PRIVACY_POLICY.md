# Privacy Policy for Potio

**Effective date:** 6 June 2026

Potio is a mixology learning, drink encyclopedia, and quiz app developed by **Mriya Interactive**.

This Privacy Policy explains how Potio handles information when you use the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

Potio does not require users to create an account, log in, or provide personal information directly to use the app.

The app may store gameplay progress locally on your device, including information such as:

* completed levels
* XP and progress
* achievements
* selected measurement units
* favourite drinks
* selected language
* sound and music preferences
* premium or no-ads status

This information is stored on your device and is used only to provide the app’s gameplay and learning features.

## 2. Advertising

Potio may show advertisements to users who have not purchased Premium or No Ads.

The app may use Google AdMob to display ads. Google AdMob may collect or process certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app activity, approximate location, and technical information, depending on your device settings and Google’s advertising policies.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services here:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Users who purchase Premium or No Ads will not see ads in Potio.

## 3. In-App Purchases

Potio may offer in-app purchases, including:

* No Ads
* Premium

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Potio does not collect or store your payment card details. Payment processing is handled by Google Play according to Google’s own policies.

## 4. Educational Content and Alcohol Disclaimer

Potio provides educational information about drinks, recipes, ingredients, and bartender knowledge.

The app does not encourage irresponsible alcohol use. Users should follow the legal drinking age and alcohol laws in their country or region.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Potio does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can review and address the issue.

## 6. Data Sharing

Potio does not sell your personal information.

Some third-party services used by the app, such as Google AdMob and Google Play Billing, may process data as necessary to provide advertising, purchase, fraud prevention, analytics, and platform services.

## 7. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay progress, favourites, unit settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Third-party services used by the app may process data according to their own privacy and security practices.

## 8. User Choices

You can control certain data and app behaviour by:

* changing unit settings in the app
* disabling music or sound effects in the app
* purchasing Premium or No Ads to remove advertising
* clearing app data from your Android device settings
* uninstalling the app

Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may delete locally stored progress.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted by updating this file or the app’s privacy policy page.
